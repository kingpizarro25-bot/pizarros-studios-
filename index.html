Execution standard

From problem to proof.

The process is deliberately less glamorous than “AI transformation.” It exists to stop prototypes from being confused with finished products.

DefineClarify the user, problem, desired outcome, and what “working” means.
DesignChoose the smallest useful workflow, integration, or product path.
BuildCreate the working version without hiding missing dependencies behind mock behavior.
VerifyTest normal use, edge cases, security concerns, and end-to-end behavior.
ProveAttach claims to evidence, document limits, and decide whether it is ready to pilot or release.
Proof standard

No invented validation.

Pizarro Studios separates what was designed, what was built, what was tested, what was adopted, and what produced a measured result. Work-in-progress stays visible where useful, but it is not presented as customer success.

STATUSIdea, prototype, MVP, pilot, deployed, or validated.
EVIDENCETests, screenshots, demos, logs, deployments, or stakeholder feedback.
LIMITSKnown blockers and unverified assumptions are stated plainly.
SECURITYCredentials, private prompts, internal controls, and sensitive data stay private.
