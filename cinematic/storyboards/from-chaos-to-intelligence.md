# From Chaos to Intelligence
### Pizarro Studios brand film · 40 seconds · 12 shots

**What the film says without saying it:** a business is full of information that does not talk
to itself. Then something starts connecting it. By the end, the same complexity is still there,
but it is under control.

**No voiceover. No explaining text until the last shot.**

Everything below is written in plain language, except the box marked **Generation prompt** in
each shot. That box is written in the technical wording the AI generators respond to — it is
machine input, not something you need to read. Copy it as-is.

---

## The four acts

| Act | Shots | Time | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Fragmentation | 1–3 | 0:00–0:09.5 | Controlled tension. Things are disconnected, not stupid. |
| 2 — Connection | 4–6 | 0:09.5–0:19.5 | Something is starting to link up. |
| 3 — Intelligence | 7–10 | 0:19.5–0:31.5 | It is a whole system, and a person is still in charge. |
| 4 — Control | 11–12 | 0:31.5–0:40 | Calm. Organised. Name. |

**Continuity to protect across all 12 shots:** the same office, the same night-into-dawn light,
the same brushed-metal-and-black materials, and one thin cyan line that behaves identically
every time it appears.

# ACT 1 — FRAGMENTATION

## Shot 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it exists** | Open on the problem: information exists, but it is a pile. |
| **What we see** | Paper documents scattered across a dark desk, overlapping at odd angles. Some pages are half-lit, some fall away into black. Fine paper texture, a bent corner, one page slightly out of the stack. Nobody is present. |
| **Where** | A dark office desk before sunrise. Only a sliver of the room is visible. |
| **Subject** | The documents themselves. |
| **Camera position** | Slightly above the desk, looking down at a shallow angle — almost skimming the surface. |
| **Lens** | 100mm close-up. Only two or three pages are sharp; the rest dissolves. |
| **Camera movement** | Glides very slowly to the right, about a hand's width across the whole shot. |
| **Light** | One hard light from the low left, out of frame. Everything to the right falls into pure black. No fill light at all. |
| **Colour** | Cold grey and dead paper white. **No cyan anywhere.** A faint dull yellow at the far edge. |
| **Length** | 3.0s |
| **How it ends** | Straight cut. |
| **Sound** | A single sheet of paper shifting. Distant air conditioning. Nothing rhythmic. |

**Generation prompt**
```
Extreme close-up macro shot, 100mm lens, scattered white paper documents overlapping at
irregular angles on a dark graphite desk surface, shallow depth of field with only two pages in
```

# ACT 2 — CONNECTION

## Shot 4

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it exists** | **The turn.** The first sign of intelligence. The line is born here. |
| **What we see** | A single thin cyan line appears as a point on the far edge of the desk and draws itself along the desk's edge toward us, hugging the surface exactly. As it passes one document, that document's edge catches the light and straightens. |
| **Where** | Same desk. Very tight. |
| **Subject** | The line. |
| **Camera position** | Almost at desk level, so the desk edge runs across the frame as a horizon. |
| **Lens** | 100mm close-up. |
| **Camera movement** | Camera slides slowly left, travelling with the line but slower than it, so the line pulls ahead. |
| **Light** | The line itself is the brightest thing in frame. Everything else stays in Shot 1's hard side light. |
| **Colour** | **First true cyan of the film.** Everything else stays cold grey. |
| **Length** | 3.0s |
| **How it ends** | Straight cut as the line leaves frame left. |
| **Sound** | The low pulse enters here, very quiet. One dry click when the line touches the document. |

**Generation prompt**
```
Extreme close-up macro, 100mm lens, a single thin bright cyan line of light drawing itself along
the machined edge of a dark graphite desk, the line hugs the physical surface exactly and does
not float, one white paper document edge catching the cyan light as the line passes it, deep
black background, hard side light from low left unchanged, camera at desk level sliding slowly
left, cinematic, photographic, shallow depth of field, high dynamic range, film grain
```
**Avoid:** the line floating in air, glowing particles, sparks, multiple lines, neon spread,
light bloom flooding the frame, holograms, circuit board patterns, blue tint on the whole image.

# ACT 3 — INTELLIGENCE

## Shot 12 — Title card

**Do not generate this shot.** Build it in the video editor so the type is pixel-sharp and the
name is exactly right. AI generators warp lettering.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why it exists** | Sign the work. |
| **What we see** | Pure black. `PIZARRO // STUDIOS` fades up, centred, capital letters, widely spaced, small in frame — `PIZARRO` in off-white, `// STUDIOS` in cyan. It holds. It fades. Then three lines fade up in small grey capitals: **AI IMPLEMENTATION / AUTOMATION / INTELLIGENT SYSTEMS.** Hold. Fade to black. |
| **Length** | 4.5s — name up 0.3s, hold 1.4s, out 0.3s, gap 0.2s, three lines up 0.3s, hold 1.5s, out 0.5s |
| **Sound** | Near silence. The low tone decays to nothing under the last line. |

Exact specification:
- Background `#05070a`
- `PIZARRO` in `#edf4f5`, `// STUDIOS` in `#55d8e8`
- Descriptor lines in `#8a9aa0`
- Monospaced typeface, letter spacing about 0.13em, all capitals
- Name sized at roughly 4% of frame height. Small. Confidence is quiet.
- No animation beyond straight fades.

## Continuity checklist — check before approving any shot

- [ ] Is the black actually black, with shadows left alone?
- [ ] Is there exactly one light source you could point at?
- [ ] Is cyan a thin line or edge — never a mist, never the whole frame?
- [ ] Is amber only where a human is?
- [ ] Does the camera move less than a third of the frame?
- [ ] Does the cyan line touch a real surface rather than float?
- [ ] Does the shot match Shot 1's materials — graphite, aluminium, paper, dark glass?
- [ ] Would this frame pass the banned list in section 12 of the rulebook?
